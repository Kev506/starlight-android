Starlight is an implementation based on the excellent "moonlight-android." It now supports game details provided by IGDB and has been updated to use AndroidX and "Compose for TV." Additionally, the layouts have been revamped. Due to ongoing development, Android API 29 or higher is required; older versions are not supported.

The app is completely free—with no advertisements, in-app purchases, or "Pro" versions.

This app allows you to stream games, programs, or your entire desktop from an NVIDIA GameStream-compatible PC on your local network, using either NVIDIA GeForce Experience or Sunshine.
Inputs from your mouse, keyboard, and controller are transmitted from your Android device to the PC.

Streaming performance may vary depending on the device used and your network configuration. HDR support requires an HDR10-capable device, a GTX 1000-series (or newer) GPU, and a game that supports HDR10. '''Features'''
* Open-source and completely free (no ads, in-app purchases, or "Pro" versions)
* Stream games purchased from any store
* Works over your local network
* Streaming at up to 4K, 120 FPS, and HDR, with 7.1 surround sound
* Keyboard and mouse support (best experienced on Android 10 or newer)
* Support for PS3, PS4, Xbox 360, Xbox One, and Android gamepads
* Force feedback support
* Local co-op mode with up to 4 connected controllers
* Mouse control via gamepad by long-pressing the Start button

'''PC Requirements'''
* NVIDIA GeForce GTX/RTX or NVIDIA Quadro GPU
* Windows 7 or newer
* NVIDIA GeForce Experience or NVIDIA Quadro Experience installed
* Sunshine

'''Quick Setup Guide'''
# Ensure that GeForce Experience or Quadro Experience is open on your PC. Enable "GameStream" in the SHIELD settings.
# Select your PC in Starlight and enter the displayed PIN on your PC.
# The database will now be updated—please wait for the process to complete.
# Start streaming! For an optimal experience, you will need a mid-to-high-end Wi-Fi router that provides a strong wireless connection to your Android device (5 GHz Wi-Fi is highly recommended). Additionally, a stable connection between your PC and the router is required (an Ethernet connection is highly recommended).
